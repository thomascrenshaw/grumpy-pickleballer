🥒 Team Match

Two teams of four. Every round, each team splits into two pairs and plays the other team on both courts. Most games wins; tied, and it comes down to singles. Major League Pickleball style, minus the money.

The teams

Skill levels from Round Robin

Auto-create balances the teams with the levels you set in Round Robin’s Skill balance box (untagged counts as Steady). Each round also leans toward even games.

Tap a name on each team to trade them. Tap two teammates in a round to change who partners whom in that round.

How it works. Four players can split into pairs three ways, so three rounds means everybody partners each teammate once. Each round, the page picks the split for each team and who faces whom, spreading out opponents and keeping games even when skill levels are set. Scores are optional and partial scores are fine. A game counts toward the match once both scores are in, and a round with any score is locked, so New shuffle only rebuilds the rounds after it. If the games end tied, a singles box shows up: play it however you agree on the day (one game, skinny singles, tag team) and just record the score.
Your names stay on your phone. Teams, rounds and scores are saved only in this browser, on this device. Nothing is sent to this site or anywhere else, and there are no accounts. A session link (team-match#play=Name,Name,…, eight names, first four are one team) fills in the teams; the names ride after the #, which browsers never send to the site, and the page clears them from the address once they're loaded.
