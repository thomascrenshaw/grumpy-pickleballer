Your names stay on your phone. The roster and schedule are saved only in this browser, on this device (most likely your phone at the court). Nothing is sent to this site or anywhere else, and there are no accounts. Clear your browser data and they're gone. Open the page on another device and it starts empty.
Using an AI assistant? If yours can see your Pickleheads sessions, it can build a session link like grumpypickleballer.com/round-robin#play=Tom%20C,Kristen%20L,… Open it and the page fills in who's playing, matches names already on your list, adds only the new ones, and asks before treating “Thomas C” as your “Tom C.” The names ride after the #, which browsers never send to the site, and the page clears them from the address once they're loaded. The assistant sees the names while it builds the link; the page itself still keeps them only on your device.
How it decides. Each round, whoever has sat out the least sits first, and nobody sits twice in a row if it can be avoided. Then it tries a pile of shuffles and keeps the one with the fewest repeat partners. Each round has its own Copy button, so you can post rounds to the group chat one at a time instead of yelling them across the courts. Rotating partners also spreads out who you play against. Fixed partners uses the standard circle rotation, so every team sees every other team before anyone repeats.
