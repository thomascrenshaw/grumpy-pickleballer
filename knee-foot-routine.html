🥒 Knee & Foot Routine

Bottom-to-top, slow-to-fast. Not medical advice — just what keeps me on the court.

⚡ Pre-Game Warm-Up ~6 min, courtside

Dynamic only. Never static-stretch cold knees.

#MoveDose
1Ankle circles + foot rolls10 each way; roll each foot 30s
2Leg swings (front-back, side-side)10 each / leg
3Walking lunges8 / leg
4Bodyweight squats10 (slow down, quick up)
5Lateral shuffles20 ft out + back × 3
6Calf raises15
7Skip / jog the baseline30s
🦵 Daily Knee Stability ~8 min

The long game. Tendon + joint resilience.

#MoveDoseWhy
1Spanish squat / wall sit3 × 30sPatellar tendon loading
2Single-leg balance30s / leg (progress: eyes closed)Proprioception
3Tibialis raises (toes up, wall)2 × 20Deceleration control
4Calf raises (straight, then bent-knee)3 × 15Both calf muscles
5Glute bridges2 × 15Weak glutes = knee pain
6Towel scrunches / toe spreads2 × 15Foot intrinsics
🧵 Hamstring Mobility daily + post-ride

Cyclist's blind spot. Strong but short hamstrings mistrack the knee. Cheap, no downside on a grumpy knee.

#MoveDoseWhen
1Foam roll hamstrings60–90s / legAfter every ride
2Supine strap/band hamstring stretch30s × 2 / legDaily
3Standing hip hinge (hands slide down legs)8–10 repsDaily
4Doorframe single-leg hamstring stretch30s / legAs needed
Self-check: film yourself coming down a flight of stairs. Stiff-legged, same leg leading, or controlled-fall = a tracking/flexibility tell worth showing your PT.
🎯 Band Strength 2–3× / week

Loop band + tube band covers all of it.

#MoveDoseTarget
1Monster walks10 steps each wayGlute med, knee tracking
2Lateral band walks10 each wayHip stability
3Banded TKEs (straighten knee)3 × 15VMO / knee stability
4Banded ankle dorsiflexion + inv/eversion2 × 15 eachAnkle = first knee defense
5Banded squats3 × 12Quads, glutes
6Clamshells2 × 15 / sideGlute med
📝 Notes
• Band days fold in the daily work — calf raises, glutes overlap on purpose. Don't double up; skip the dupes.
• Sharp pain = stop & assess. Dull tendon-loading ache is fine; sharp is a "see someone" signal, not a "push through" one.
⭐ Minimum viable day: Spanish squat + single-leg balance + calf raises. Do those even when short on time.
Pairs with the animated warm-up. Green moves are knee-friendly; deeper-loading ones (lunges, squats, skip) go gentle on a grumpy day.
