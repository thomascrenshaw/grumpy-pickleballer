🥒 Stacking

Keep both forehands in the middle, or hide a weaker wing on the sideline.

Stacking means both partners set up on the same side of the court before the serve, then cross to their preferred sides once the ball's in play. The payoff is keeping both forehands covering the middle (where most balls go), or tucking a weaker backhand out on the sideline.
