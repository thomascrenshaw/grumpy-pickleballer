🥒 Dynamic Warm-Up

Meet Mr. PBSF. Move through range, don't hold. Never static-stretch a cold knee. ~6–8 min courtside.

Start here. Grumpy knee today? Stay in Knee-Friendly — only the joint-kind moves. Feeling good? Tap Full Warm-Up for the deeper-loading ones too.

Knee-friendly Deeper load — go gentle ⏸ Pause motion

Leg Swings — Front / Back

10 each leg

Hip flexors & hamstrings. Hold something for balance. Controlled — don't force the top.

Leg Swings — Side to Side

10 each leg

The hip in-&-out flexors. Preps the lateral movement pickleball lives on. (Yes, that's the "Alive" pose.)

Lateral Shuffles

20 ft out & back × 3

Low athletic stance, push off the outside foot. The most sport-specific warm-up move for pickleball.

Arm Circles + Shoulder Rolls

10 each way

Preps the paddle shoulder. Big to small, both directions.

Torso Rotations

10 each way

Rotational prep for serves & drives. Loose and controlled — don't crank it cold.

Ankle Circles + Calf Raises

10 circles + 15 raises

Ankle = the knee's first defense, and calf raises double as plantar/heel prep. Always do these.

Walking Lunges

8 each leg

Front knee over ankle, not past toes. Deeper knee load — hidden on knee days. Sub step-back lunges to ease the joint.

Bodyweight Squats

10 — slow down, quick up

Full-depth wakes up quads/glutes but loads the knee. Grumpy day → keep them shallow or stay knee-friendly.
