🥒 Grumpy Pickleballer

Play smart, protect the joints, let them make the mistakes. A field guide from a guy who won't stop playing.

The score doesn't really matter (it just tells me who's serving). I put this site together to help you warm up without wrecking your knees (like I did), move so the court does the work instead of your body, keep your head right (aka keep your mental game sharp), and maybe pick up something that helps your game.
