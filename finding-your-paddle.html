🥒 Finding Your Paddle

The best paddle isn't the one you see the pros use. Try a few before you buy one.

The best paddle isn't the one you see the pros use. The best paddle is the one you feel works for you.

Try others first

  1. Borrow one for a game.Ask another player if you can swap for a game. Most people are happy to, and a full game tells you more than any review.
  2. Look for demos.See if there are demo paddles in your area. Plenty of brands and shops will lend one out before you buy.
  3. Find the ambassadors.A lot of brands have local ambassadors, and they usually carry paddles you can try.

What to notice when you try one

NoticeWhat it tells you
Grip sizeCan you hold it loosely and still control it? Too big or too small and you'll squeeze without meaning to.
WeightHeavier feels steadier on blocks and drives. Lighter feels quicker in fast exchanges at the kitchen.
ShapeA wider face is more forgiving. An elongated one gives you reach and power, with a smaller sweet spot.
FeelSome faces pop off the paddle; some hold the ball longer for control. Neither is better. One will suit you.

One game isn't a verdict. Every new paddle feels strange for the first few points. If one feels close, try it again before you decide.
